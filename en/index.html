01 · Features

Everything you need to understand electricity, on one board.

From a lamp and a switch to an industrial control panel and a PLC program, Tayaar simulates the circuit the way it works in reality.

ELECTRONICS

Electronics library

Resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors and ICs, simulated over time by an analog solver.

INDUSTRIAL

Industrial control panels

Contactors, thermal relays and timers: direct-on-line, forward/reverse and sequential start.

SOLAR

Solar power

PV arrays, batteries and inverters: grid-tied, off-grid and hybrid systems, with irradiance you can change while the simulation runs.

PLC · LADDER

PLC ladder editor

Write ladder logic with contacts, coils, timers and counters, link it to the electrical circuit and watch it run step by step.

RESIDENTIAL

House wiring

Lamps, switches, two-way staircase lighting, breakers, RCDs and a house board. Watch the breaker trip on leakage or overload.

LESSONS

Lessons the engine checks

Start from an incomplete circuit, finish the tasks, then press “Check my circuit”: the simulator runs it and tells you what passed, what failed and why.

CALCULATORS

Wiring calculators

Total load, cable size and breaker, voltage drop, and a panel schedule with phase balancing, exported to PDF and CSV.

AI ASSISTANT

AI assistant Coming soon

An assistant that explains your circuit, sizes cables, checks voltage drop and runs the simulation on a copy of your project to answer your questions. Included in the Pro + AI plan.

06 · FAQ

Questions everyone asks.

Does Tayaar work offline?

Yes. The simulation, calculators and lessons all run on your computer without a connection.

Does Tayaar replace a qualified electrician?

No. Tayaar is a teaching and preliminary-planning tool and its results are simplified. Real installations require a qualified electrician and compliance with the local electrical code.

Why does Windows warn me during installation?

Because the installer is not code-signed yet. Check the SHA-256 hash, then choose More info and Run anyway.

What is the difference between the plans?

Free gives you some lessons and components, without saving projects. Pro unlocks every simulation tool. Pro + AI adds the AI assistant.

When will subscriptions be available?

Soon. Prices and payment details will be announced here; message us on WhatsApp to register your interest.

Is my data kept when I update?

Yes. A new version replaces the old one, and your projects, settings and lesson progress stay as they are.

Licences and third-party components
